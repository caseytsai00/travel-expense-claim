**Casey** · In person · 02:41 PM

What evidence are you checking for and how do you check it?

**Travel Administration Lead** · In person · 02:41 PM

When an employee submits a travel claim, I check identity, payment, deadlines, and expense eligibility before calculating any reimbursement entitlement. Specifically, I compare the claim against receipt cards from the Receipt binder and separate settled employee-to-merchant transactions from the Merchant payments tab in our Travel registers workbook to ensure that every claimed cost has both a matching receipt and settled merchant proof. A receipt by itself does not prove payment. I also verify that the employee and trip identities match, check that the filing date falls within two calendar months after the trip end, and apply category-specific rules such as multiplying documented lodging nights or meal days by their applicable destination caps. If any evidence, receipt, settled merchant proof, or required FX rate is missing, the line remains unresolved rather than assumed to be zero.

**Casey** · In person · 03:23 PM

What happens if a line is labeled "unresolved"?

**Travel Administration Lead** · In person · 03:23 PM

When a line is labeled unresolved, it means we cannot process or pay it until the missing fact, definition, or evidence is supplied. An unresolved line is never treated as a zero by assumption. For example, if a category is unknown, we must obtain its applicable policy definition. If an applicable lodging or meals cap, documented units, required FX rate, receipt, or settled merchant payment proof is missing, that gap must be resolved by getting the proper evidence or definition from the employee or Finance. Unresolved items stay held rather than being silently bypassed or approved.

Yes, employees may initiate the cancellation of an exact trip or permit version independently of any claim. **Travel Administration Lead** · In person · 11:28 PM

When a cancellation is requested while a claim is still pending, a valid requested cancellation pauses new affected arrangements and payment readiness pending the supervisor's confirmation, without changing actual money or Finance request or commitment status.
